# 60-Day Job Switch Roadmap

A day-by-day plan to go from "stuck and regretting" to "signed offer" in 60 days, built around this repo's 50-projects-in-50-days challenge.

---

## Read This First: Breaking the Regret Cycle

Regret is your brain replaying decisions you can no longer change. You cannot think your way out of it — you can only **act** your way out. This roadmap is designed so that every single day has one small, finishable task. Finishing things daily is what rewires the loop.

**The 5 rules (non-negotiable):**

1. **The past is data, not debt.** When a regret thought shows up ("I should have switched years ago"), translate it into one line of data: *"I want a better job. That's why I'm doing today's task."* Then do the task.
2. **One day = one project = one green square.** Your only job today is today's box. Not the whole roadmap, not your career, just today's box.
3. **No zero days.** Even on your worst day, do 15 minutes. A 15-minute day keeps the chain alive; a zero day feeds the regret loop.
4. **Compare only to yesterday's you.** Not to people on LinkedIn, not to ex-colleagues who switched earlier. Your commit history is your only scoreboard.
5. **Decide once, then stop re-deciding.** You've decided: 60 days, this plan. Re-litigating the decision every night IS the regret cycle. The decision is made. Execute.

**When you spiral (it will happen):** Stand up, open this repo, pick the smallest task on today's list, set a 25-minute timer, start. Motion kills rumination.

## The Big Picture

| Phase | Days | Focus | Job-hunt milestone |
|-------|------|-------|--------------------|
| 1. Build momentum | 1–14 | Projects 1–14, daily shipping habit | Resume drafted, LinkedIn updated |
| 2. Build proof | 15–30 | Projects 15–30, portfolio site live | Applications begin (5/week) |
| 3. Build interview muscle | 31–45 | Projects 31–42 + interview prep | 10 applications/week, first interviews |
| 4. Close | 46–60 | Projects 43–50 + heavy interviewing | Negotiate and sign |

**Daily time budget (2.5–4 hrs):**
- 60–90 min → today's project (code it, push it, deploy it)
- 30–60 min → job-hunt task of the day (see phases below)
- 30 min → learning/interview prep
- 10 min → log what you did (one line in `PROGRESS.md`) — this is your anti-regret journal

---

## Phase 1 — Momentum (Days 1–14)

**Goal:** Re-establish identity as someone who ships daily. Get your paperwork ready.

**Projects:** Do projects 1–14 of the 50-days challenge (Expanding Cards, Progress Steps, Rotating Nav, Hidden Search, Blurry Loading, Scroll Animation, Split Landing Page, Form Wave, Sound Board, Dad Jokes, Event KeyCodes, FAQ Collapse, Random Choice Picker, Animated Nav). One folder per project, pushed the same day.

**Job-hunt tasks:**
- **Day 1–2:** Write down your target — role title, 3 must-haves, salary floor, locations/remote. Write it at the top of `PROGRESS.md`. This kills the "but what do I even want" spiral.
- **Day 3–5:** Rewrite your resume. One page. Every bullet = action + technology + measurable result. Tailor it to the target role.
- **Day 6–7:** Update LinkedIn + GitHub profile README. Headline says the role you WANT, not the one you have. Turn on "Open to Work" (recruiters only).
- **Day 8–10:** List 30 target companies. For each: careers page link + one person you could reach out to.
- **Day 11–14:** Reconnect with 5 former colleagues/friends in the industry. Simple message: what you're looking for, one specific ask.

**Exit criteria:** 14 projects pushed, resume done, LinkedIn done, 30-company list done.

---

## Phase 2 — Proof (Days 15–30)

**Goal:** Public evidence you can build. Applications flowing.

**Projects:** Projects 15–30. Start deploying every project (GitHub Pages / Netlify / Vercel — pick one, stick with it).

**Job-hunt tasks:**
- **Day 15–18:** Build a simple portfolio site (this can count as extra credit alongside the daily project). Home page: who you are, top 6 projects with live links, contact. Deploy it.
- **Day 19+:** **Apply to 5 jobs/week minimum.** Tailor the first 3 resume bullets per application; don't rewrite the whole thing. Track every application in a simple sheet: company, date, status, contact.
- Every application to a target company → also message one human there (recruiter or engineer). Referrals convert ~10x better than cold applications.
- **Day 25–30:** Write 2 short posts (LinkedIn or dev.to) about something you built in the challenge. Visibility compounds.

**Exit criteria:** 30 projects pushed, portfolio live, 10+ applications sent, tracking sheet running.

---

## Phase 3 — Interview Muscle (Days 31–45)

**Goal:** Convert applications into interviews, interviews into onsites.

**Projects:** Projects 31–42. If interview prep is eating time, it's OK to simplify a day's project — shipping something small beats skipping.

**Job-hunt tasks:**
- **Raise applications to 10/week.** Follow up on every application older than 7 days with one polite message.
- **Daily 30-min prep rotation:**
  - Mon/Wed/Fri — technical: JS fundamentals (closures, promises, event loop, `this`), CSS layout, DOM manipulation. One coding problem/day (easy → medium).
  - Tue/Thu — behavioral: write STAR stories. You need 6: a hard bug, a conflict, a failure, a thing you led, a thing you learned fast, why you're switching. Say them out loud.
  - Sat — mock interview (friend, or record yourself answering 3 questions).
- **Prepare your "why are you switching" answer.** Forward-looking, zero bitterness: *"I've grown as far as I can in my current role and I want X, which this job has."* Never badmouth the current job — and notice this answer is also the antidote to your regret loop: the past taught you what you want.

**Exit criteria:** 42 projects, 30+ total applications, 6 STAR stories written, at least 2 interviews scheduled or done.

---

## Phase 4 — Close (Days 46–60)

**Goal:** Finish the challenge. Land the offer.

**Projects:** Projects 43–50. **Day 50: challenge complete — post about it.** A finished 50-day streak is a genuinely strong signal to employers; make sure it's visible (pinned repo, LinkedIn post, portfolio link).

**Job-hunt tasks:**
- Interviews are now the priority; projects drop to maintenance mode after day 50.
- After every interview, write 3 lines: what went well, what didn't, one thing to fix. Fix it before the next one.
- **Keep applying until you sign.** A verbal "we like you" is not an offer. An email offer is not a signed offer.
- **Negotiate.** When the offer comes: thank them, ask for 24–48 hours, counter once with a specific number backed by your market research. The worst realistic outcome is they say "the offer stands."
- If you have momentum but no offer by day 60: you are not failing — you have a portfolio, a network, interview reps, and a pipeline. The average search takes longer; days 61+ are just more of Phase 4. The system keeps working; keep feeding it.

**Exit criteria:** 50/50 projects. Signed offer, or an active pipeline of late-stage interviews.

## Weekly Review (every Sunday, 20 minutes)

1. Projects shipped this week: __ / 7
2. Applications sent: __ / target
3. Replies / interviews: __
4. One thing to change next week: __
5. One line of gratitude to past-you for the work done this week. (Yes, really. It's the direct counter-programming to regret.)

## Anti-Regret Emergency Card

Stuck in the loop right now? Do these in order:

1. Close the tab/app where the spiral started.
2. Open this repo.
3. Do today's project for 25 minutes (timer on).
4. Push the commit.
5. Look at your commit graph. That green is proof the loop is lying to you.

**You don't need to feel ready. You need to do Day 1.**
